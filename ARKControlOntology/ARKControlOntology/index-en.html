Any measure or action that modifies or regulates risk. Controls include any policy, procedure, practice, process, technology, technique, method, or device that modifies or regulates risk. They can exist or be required as additional in order to further mitigate the risk. Risk treatments become controls, or modify existing controls, once they are implemented.
